# India EV Sales Analytics Dashboard (2022–2024)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Excel-based analytics solution to analyze India’s Electric Vehicle (EV) market across states, vehicle categories (2-wheelers and 4-wheelers), and manufacturers. The dashboard leverages PivotTables, Power Pivot (DAX), and advanced visualizations to deliver actionable insights on EV adoption and growth trends.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze EV sales performance from 2022 to 2024  
- Compare 2W and 4W category growth and penetration  
- Evaluate state-wise and manufacturer-wise EV adoption  
- Calculate CAGR, market share, and revenue growth  
- Forecast EV sales for 2030 using CAGR-based modeling  

---

## 📊 Key Features
- Interactive Excel dashboards with slicers (State, Category, Maker)  
- EV penetration rate and market share analysis  
- CAGR analysis for EV and total vehicle sales  
- Monthly and quarterly trend visualizations  
- State-wise EV sales comparison and projections  
- Manufacturer performance and market share insights  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel  
- PivotTables & PivotCharts  
- Power Pivot (Data Model)  
- DAX Measures  
- Advanced Excel Visualizations
